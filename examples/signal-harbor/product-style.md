# Product-style derivation

| Product evidence | UI consequence | Pending verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Responders monitor multiple active incidents under time pressure. | Use a dense desktop information hierarchy with a scannable incident queue and persistent operational context. | Verify hierarchy and scan speed in the real 1440×900 browser render. |
| Severity, ownership, and lifecycle status must be distinguished quickly. | Use restrained dark operational surfaces with explicit text labels and status tokens; color must never be the sole signal. | Measure contrast and inspect status differentiation. |
| Assignment and resolution are consequential actions. | Give each a focused step with clear confirmation, validation, and status feedback instead of ambiguous inline toggles. | Exercise happy, blocked, failed, and retry paths. |
| Responders may work without a pointing device. | Use semantic controls, logical tab order, deterministic post-action focus, and strongly visible focus indicators. | Complete keyboard and focus checks. |
| State changes may not otherwise be visually discoverable. | Announce assignment, errors, retry recovery, and resolution through a polite live region. | Inspect assistive status behavior in the real implementation. |
| Operational interfaces should avoid distracting animation. | Keep transitions brief and functional, and remove nonessential motion when reduced motion is requested. | Verify the reduced-motion media path. |
| The target has no supplied source brand. | Use neutral product-specific typography, spacing, components, and language; do not invent brand provenance. | Record any future source-brand input before changing the system. |

## Intentionally excluded

- No palette, typography, material, layout, component, edge, decoration, or placement rule comes from the character or composition images.
- No universal card grid, speech-bubble system, decorative mascot corner, or composition-derived scenery is authorized.
- Visual direction remains a pending implementation contract until required visual grounding, real rendering, comparison, and repair are complete.
