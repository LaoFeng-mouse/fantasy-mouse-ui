# Product-style derivation

This interface system is derived only from the user-specified regional botany event, its public website surface, the itinerary-planning workflow, and accessibility requirements. Fantasy Mouse assets and bundled compositions provide no evidence for layout, palette, typography, material, component, title, spacing, border, shadow, or placement decisions.

## Product rationale

- **Surface and users:** A responsive public website for visitors who may be casually browsing outdoors, planning ahead at home, or checking a saved itinerary on a phone.
- **Frequency and density:** Episodic event planning with a moderately dense program; scanning day, time, habitat, venue, and availability must be faster than reading full descriptions.
- **Tone and trust:** Organic editorial warmth for regional botany, paired with precise schedule and conflict language.
- **Brand evidence:** No supplied visual brand system. The event subject supports a field-publication vocabulary, but not imitation of the character compositions.
- **Color semantics:** Deep leaf and ink neutrals establish hierarchy; a warm flower or clay accent identifies actions; conflict uses a distinct high-contrast warning treatment with text and icon support.
- **Type and spacing:** A readable editorial serif may distinguish headlines and botanical stories; a robust sans serif handles controls, times, venues, and status. Spacing follows a compact program rhythm with larger breaks between editorial sections.
- **Materials and edges:** Flat, calm reading surfaces with subtle rules and restrained rounding. Botanical texture belongs only to content imagery, never behind dense controls or critical copy.
- **Motion:** Brief filter and drawer transitions clarify continuity; reduced-motion mode removes translation and uses immediate state changes.
- **Accessibility:** Semantic headings and landmarks, persistent visible focus, named filters, dialog labelling and focus return, live status announcements, non-color conflict cues, adequate contrast, and comfortable pointer targets.

## Coherence and exclusions

The interface must remain coherent if the character assets are hidden: hierarchy, filters, cards, conflict recovery, saved feedback, and responsive behavior still derive from the festival workflow. Do not import the bundled compositions' paper texture, taped labels, thick black outlines, window chrome, block title treatment, palette, shadow system, or character placement. Botanical motifs may appear only when justified by event content and must not reduce legibility or become a generic decorative layer.
