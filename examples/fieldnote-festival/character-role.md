# Character-role derivation

This artifact derives character participation only from canonical Fantasy Mouse identity and the Fieldnote Festival workflow. It does not define the website's layout, palette, typography, materials, components, or navigation.

## Stable identity and anatomy contract

Every appearance preserves the compact round grey photographic or texture-rich head, tiny horizontal ears, narrowed vacant eyes, prominent human-like toothy grin, compact white hand-drawn bean body, recognizable proportions, and earnest absurd dreamer temperament.

The default chest V/U is one clasped hand pair, not a belly mark. In an action pose it must be removed completely and replaced by exactly one coherent pair of body-connected arms and hands. Action hands attach below the head/body seam, contact the real prop, and remain clear of the eyes, teeth, ears, and silhouette.

## Participation boundaries

- Do not place the mouse in the persistent header, day-filter row, mobile navigation, plan-drawer handle, or floating assistant position.
- Do not repeat it on session cards or use it as an icon, loading indicator, status badge, error symbol, or button label.
- Keep the conflict dialog literal and character-free so times, replacement consequences, cancel recovery, and focus behavior remain authoritative.
- Keep saved confirmation understandable without character imagery. The state is conveyed by text and semantics, not expression.
- Do not reconstruct the mouse with CSS, SVG, emoji, ASCII, or placeholder geometry. A future implementation must use approved or generated raster assets and preserve one hand pair.
