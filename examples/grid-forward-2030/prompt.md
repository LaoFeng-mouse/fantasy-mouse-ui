# Grid Forward 2030 presentation prompt

Create an editable 16:9 presentation for a fictional regional-grid steering committee.

## Communication job

By the end, the committee should approve a staged 2030 modernization portfolio because the illustrative scenario shows that reliability gains require sequencing visibility, flexibility, and resilience investments together.

## Required eight-slide sequence

1. **Title** — Introduce “Grid Forward 2030” and the modernization decision horizon.
2. **Decision** — State the approval requested: authorize a staged portfolio with explicit phase gates.
3. **Gap** — Show the illustrative demand/reliability gap and why isolated projects do not close it.
4. **Priorities** — Explain three linked priorities: visibility, flexibility, and resilience.
5. **Portfolio** — Compare coherent portfolio choices and make the staged, balanced portfolio the recommended choice.
6. **Roadmap** — Sequence enabling foundations, scaled flexibility, and resilience hardening through 2030.
7. **Risks** — Pair principal delivery risks with concrete mitigations and decision gates.
8. **Approval** — Restate the decision, phase conditions, owners, and immediate next actions.

## Content integrity

- Treat the organization, portfolio, metrics, dates, and outcomes as fictional scenario content.
- Label every figure, chart, table, and quantitative callout `Illustrative planning scenario — not a forecast`.
- Do not present invented claims as external facts.
- Keep the causal argument explicit: visibility enables informed control, flexibility absorbs variability, and resilience limits the impact of disruptions; the three must be sequenced together.
- Use one clear composition per slide and only use native charts or shapes when they materially clarify the decision.

## Visual direction

Use a Swiss/data-driven presentation system derived from the committee decision task: disciplined grid, asymmetric but controlled compositions, strong typographic hierarchy, generous negative space, precise rules, restrained semantic color, and legible quantitative annotation. Avoid UI-like card grids, ornamental dashboard chrome, paper/collage styling, thick cartoon borders, and visual traits copied from the bundled character compositions.

Use at least 50 pt for the deck title, 35 pt for slide titles, 24 pt for subheads, and 16 pt for body text. Preserve contrast, reading order, editable structure, and clear source notes.

## Presenter mouse

Use the canonical Fantasy Mouse identity as a restrained presenter, never as factual authority. Include a pointer-based action pose where it clarifies a chart or sequence and one default-clasped listening state near the decision or approval moment. In action mode, remove the default chest V/U and show exactly one body-connected hand pair with real pointer contact. In default mode, the chest V/U is the only clasped hand pair. Keep the face, tiny horizontal ears, toothy grin, round grey head, white bean body, and earnest absurd temperament recognizable. Do not use a speech bubble.

## Evidence state

Prepare this as a strict public benchmark, but do not claim generation, testing, runtime, rendering, visual checking, comparison, or acceptance until those gates have real evidence.
