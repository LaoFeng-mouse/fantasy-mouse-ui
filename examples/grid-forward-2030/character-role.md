# Character-role translation

The Fantasy Mouse is a restrained presenter and listening proxy for the committee. It adds continuity and directs attention; it never supplies evidence, endorses invented facts, or determines the slide design. The optional bubble is excluded throughout because the decision narrative and figure labels must remain literal.

Stable identity in every appearance: compact round grey texture-rich head, tiny horizontal ears, narrowed vacant eyes, prominent human-like toothy grin, compact white hand-drawn bean body, and earnest absurd dreamer temperament.

## Anatomy and compositing rules

- Default and action hands are mutually exclusive.
- In action mode, delete the chest V/U before adding arms and hands.
- Use exactly one coherent hand pair with forearms attached below the grey-head/white-body seam.
- Ensure the pointer is held at a real grip point and contacts the intended datum, relationship, option, or phase.
- Keep limbs clear of the eyes, teeth, ears, and defining silhouette.
- Preserve readable body/arms/hands/prop occlusion at final presentation size.
- The bundled composition examples have no slide-style authority and their chest-hand conflict must not be reproduced.
